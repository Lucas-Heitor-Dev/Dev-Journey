# MATH

Mathematics foundations for computer science, machine learning and AI engineering.

## Folders

- [ALGEBRA-PRECALCULUS](ALGEBRA-PRECALCULUS/): Algebra and precalculus.
- [CALCULUS](CALCULUS/): Differential and integral calculus.
- [DISCRETE-MATH](DISCRETE-MATH/): Logic, sets, combinatorics and graph theory.
- [LINEAR-ALGEBRA](LINEAR-ALGEBRA/): Vectors, matrices and transformations.
- [OPTIMIZATION](OPTIMIZATION/): Optimization methods and applications.
- [PROBABILITY-STATISTICS](PROBABILITY-STATISTICS/): Probability and statistical foundations.
